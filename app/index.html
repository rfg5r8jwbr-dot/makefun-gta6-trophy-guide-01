Road to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours, no online trophies, no difficulty setting, no mission replay. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter.

Watch out — one possibly missable trophy. Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) may need Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation at Jason’s house, which disappears after Chapter 1. Do it the morning after the mission Down to Earth: talk to Lucia in the kitchen, follow her to the balcony, choose Accept. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story.

About this guide

Names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them. Every guide is written for this page in our own words; each one names its sources beside it, so you can check where every fact came from — a location or step with no source earns no trust, so none is given without one. The one trophy whose method is still unconfirmed at launch, Bad Luck, I Guess, says so plainly instead of guessing. Progress is stored only in this browser’s localStorage; export and import move it as a file. No Rockstar artwork, logo or game screenshot is used anywhere: the look is our own.
